Two-dimensional TTC

Future oriented footprints are propagated with velocity and acceleration. TTC is the earliest future instant at which their rectangles intersect.

02

Event-based PET

PET is the gap from the first user completely leaving the shared zone to the second user entering it. Overlapping occupancy produces zero.

03

Anti-tunneling physics

Each visual frame is split into small adaptive physics steps, capped by object size and speed, with collision tests at every internal step.
