# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a constructive environment for researchers, engineers, students, practitioners, and community contributors.

Participants are expected to communicate professionally, critique ideas rather than people, respect differing levels of experience, avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct, and give proper credit for technical and research contributions.

Unacceptable behavior includes personal attacks, threats, harassment, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private information, or knowingly submitting confidential/proprietary material without permission.

Project maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions and interactions that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in participation restrictions.

For conduct concerns, contact the project maintainer privately through an appropriate contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile rather than posting sensitive information in a public issue.
